User Guide
How to master WA Bot Dashboard

1. Accounts & Limits

Link WhatsApp accounts via "Linked Devices". Set a Daily Limit for each account to prevent spam blocks.

Proxy & IP Rotation: Assign Proxies (http://user:pass@ip:port) to each account to prevent mass-banning. Use Rotate All IPs to randomize.

2. Contacts & Groups

Go to the Contacts tab to upload a CSV/Excel file. You can also extract members from Groups using the Groups Tab. The system automatically filters out invalid numbers and Removes Duplicate Numbers so you never message the same person twice.

3. Campaigns, Files & Forwarding

Blast messages with attachments. Add multiple templates; the bot randomly picks one per recipient. You can attach files (Images, Videos, PDFs, etc.) directly to your campaign. Alternatively, you can use the Forward Catalog to seamlessly forward existing media messages directly from your WhatsApp to the recipients.

4. Anti-Ban Strategies

Set a Message Delay (e.g., 5-10s) between each message. Use the Batch/Recipients setting to pause sending after a certain number of messages (e.g., Sleep for 60 seconds after every 50 messages). This highly simulates natural human behavior.

5. Group Extraction Settings

You can select any WhatsApp group your accounts are in and extract all its members' phone numbers. When extracting numbers from groups, you can choose to Exclude Group Admins to avoid messaging group owners, significantly reducing the chances of your number being reported as spam.

6. Spintax & Auto Blacklist

Spintax: Use format {Hi|Hello|Hey} in your messages. The bot will randomly pick one option per recipient to avoid spam bans.

Auto Blacklist: If a recipient replies with STOP, they are automatically added to a Blacklist and will never receive messages from you again, even if they are in your Excel list. This protects your account from being reported.

7. Message Scheduling

You can set a future date and time for your campaigns. The bot will automatically start sending messages at the scheduled time. This is perfect for birthday wishes, reminders, and holiday greetings.

8. WhatsApp Polls

Engage with your audience by sending interactive Polls. You can create multiple options and even restrict users to selecting only one option. Polls are a great way to collect feedback quickly.

9. Analytics & Revoking Messages

The Reports Tab saves a history of all your sent campaigns. You can see real-time delivery status (Sent, Delivered, Read). If you sent a wrong message, you can use the Revoke Messages feature to delete it for everyone directly from the dashboard.
