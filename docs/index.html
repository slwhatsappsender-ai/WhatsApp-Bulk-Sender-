Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about WhatsApp Bot Desktop PRO? Find clear answers below.

Is my data and contact list secure? +
Yes, 100%. WhatsApp Bot Desktop PRO runs purely as a standalone local Windows software. All your contact numbers, message contents, and WhatsApp sessions remain strictly stored on your own PC and are never sent to external cloud servers.
How does the Anti-Ban system safeguard my numbers? +
Our Anti-Ban engine uses a multi-layered approach: humanized variable delays between messages (e.g. 4 to 10 seconds), automatic batch pauses to cool down sessions, and multi-bot round-robin rotation to spread dispatch volume evenly across up to 5 WhatsApp accounts.
What is the Zero Re-Upload Turbo Forwarding feature? +
When sending large videos, documents, or photos to thousands of contacts, standard tools re-upload the file each time, exhausting your bandwidth. Zero Re-Upload sends the file once to your own number and forwards the cached WhatsApp CDN link instantly to all recipients with zero repeated upload delay.
How does PRO license activation work? +
Inside the desktop application, navigate to the Settings tab and locate the License Key section. When you input your activation key, your computer's unique Machine ID is instantly verified offline and unlocks all 5 accounts, voice notes, stickers, polls, and delivery tracking tools immediately.
What are the system requirements? +
WhatsApp Bot Desktop PRO runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). It requires at least 4GB of RAM and an active internet connection to connect with WhatsApp servers.
